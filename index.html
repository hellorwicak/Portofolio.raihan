About

Hi, I’m Raihan Wahyu Wicaksono. You can call me Rei. I’m a student at Kalbis University, based in Jakarta. Lately, I’ve been freelancing for events, or more like event organizing, and just doing whatever side gigs to make a buck XD.

I offer my expertise to agencies and creative teams.

After a year as an Information Systems student at Kalbis University, I craved to reconnect with craftsmanship, to make beautiful, carefully designed objects.

I’m a developer with a taste for design and animation. Besides all that tech stuff, I’m actually pretty active— I Love working out and exploring new things. Always down to learn random new stuff, whether it’s academic or non-academic.
